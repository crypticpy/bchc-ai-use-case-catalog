{%- comment -%} "Is this reusable for us?" — the at-a-glance strip at the top of an entry page. Renders every field whose effective card slot is `icon` (the signal fields), then every `card: fact` field (the ones that belong here and NOT on a catalog card), plus any `card: meta` field that has not already been shown in the page header. Values carry their `option_meta` icon and `warn` tone. Facts with no value are skipped; the whole strip disappears when nothing is left. Each fact shows at most `cap` values and trails a "+n" counter — the chip idiom from entry-card.html — naming the hidden values to a screen reader. The cap is 3, not the cards' 2: this is the page's authoritative signal list, and at rail-narrowed widths a trailing "+n" wraps onto a line of its own, so a lower cap would hide a fact without buying back a row. The strip is a wrapping band of content-sized facts (CSS: .fact-strip), so nothing is padded to match a neighbour. `option_meta.description` follows the strip as ONE collapsed disclosure covering every value the entry actually carries — a `title` per value reached a hovering mouse only, and on a labelled element it double-announces.
