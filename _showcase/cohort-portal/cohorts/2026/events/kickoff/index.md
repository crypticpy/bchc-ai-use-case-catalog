## Agenda

- Welcome, and who is in the room
- How the six months run: the five clinics, the coach, the deadline
- What a finishable question looks like — three examples from the 2025 cohort, one of which was not finishable
- Data stewards: what to ask for, and when to ask
- Teams draft a first question and pick a coaching slot

## Before the next session

Bring a one-page question to the scoping clinic in February. One page is the limit, not the target: the clinic exists to cut it down, and every team so far has left with a smaller question than they arrived with.
