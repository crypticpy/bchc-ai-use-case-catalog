## What it is

One master deck and one handout, each in five languages, kept in step. The templates are not the English version run through a machine: the terminology comes from the shared glossary, a translator has reviewed each, and the layouts were rebuilt so that a heading that runs 40% longer does not push a slide out of shape.

## Who it is for

Anyone running a community session or producing a handout who would otherwise commission a translation from scratch or, more often, go ahead in English only.

## How to use it

Start from the language you need rather than translating the English deck yourself. Keep the glossary terms as they are — they are what makes materials from different teams read as coming from the same organisation. Anything you add beyond the template goes through the translation request process before it is used publicly.

## Notes from the library

The right-to-left variants are the ones worth checking on a real projector before a session; mirrored layouts still occasionally surprise a presenter who has only seen the file in a preview pane.
