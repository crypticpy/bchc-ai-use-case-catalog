## Overview

Most meeting notes are a transcript nobody reads. This template inverts the order: the first two sections are decisions made and actions agreed, each action carrying a name and a date. Discussion notes go underneath, where they are available but not in the way.

## Details

Copy it into the meeting's shared folder before the meeting, not after. Fill the decisions section live — if it is empty at the end, that is worth noticing out loud. The actions section is designed to be pasted straight into a task tracker without editing.
