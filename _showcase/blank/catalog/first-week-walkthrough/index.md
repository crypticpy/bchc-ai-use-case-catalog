## Overview

New starters used to spend three days waiting for accounts and then get handed a large piece of work with no context. The walkthrough fixes the sequencing: accounts are requested a week before the start date, introductions are scheduled rather than left to chance, and there is one small, real task ready for Wednesday.

## Details

The Wednesday task is the part that matters and the part most often skipped. It should be something genuinely useful, finishable in a day, and reviewed by a colleague — the goal is to have contributed something and met someone by the middle of the first week.

Friday is a half-hour check-in with two questions: what is still unclear, and what did the walkthrough get wrong. The second question is how this page gets updated.
