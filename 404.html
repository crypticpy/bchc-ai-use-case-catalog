A dead end is the one page where the visitor has nothing to work with, so it carries the two things that can rescue the visit: the catalog search box (the same plain GET form the home hero submits into `//?q=`, no JS) and the newest entries. Both come from _data/schema.yml — the entry path, the plural noun and the reserved `title`/`summary` keys — and both disappear with the `catalog` module.
